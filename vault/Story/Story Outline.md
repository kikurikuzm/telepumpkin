## The Premise
*Telepumpkin 2* is a simple game about a pumpkin being imbued with magic. Said pumpkin, known as '[The Pumpkin](Kin)', or '[[Kin]]', is disappointed by the fact that it has now achieved sentience. In an attempt at revenge, 'The Pumpkin' wants to destroy whoever inflicted it with such a burden.

## The Stakes
'The Pumpkin' fails to ever catch up to the individual who gave it sentience, and has to slowly and painfully rot away without any closure as to who and why this happened.

## The Core Conflict
'The Pumpkin' versus 'The Unknown', with 'The Unknown' being the thing that cursed 'The Pumpkin'.

## The Resolution
'The Pumpkin', after journeying for about 3 months has 'The Rot' begin to set in. Even though pumpkin's life was short lived, they still got to experience the ups and downs of what life has to offer, and interact with the people who you meet along the way. This sliver of life soon fades, returning everything to how it was.

They never get their revenge, but at least they had a goal throughout their short life.

## The Lesson
'The Pumpkin' learns of the beauties of life. This occurrence being such a near impossible thing has humbled the pumpkin. With this revelation, 'The Pumpkin' slowly withers away peacefully.
